BIOGRAPHY

Originally from Morón, Buenos Aires (Argentina), Parlagreco is a DJ and producer active in the electronic music scene since 2004. With more than two decades behind the decks, he has developed a distinctive sound blending Techno and Progressive House, characterized by deep grooves, hypnotic atmospheres and immersive storytelling on the dancefloor.

His musical vision has been influenced by iconic electronic artists such as Richie Hawtin, Underworld and John Digweed, shaping his approach to building dynamic and emotionally driven DJ sets.

Throughout his career, Parlagreco has performed in cities such as Buenos Aires, Milan, Barcelona, Amsterdam and Copenhagen, connecting with audiences across both the South American and European electronic scenes.

He has shared the booth with respected artists including Jorge Ciccioli, Emi Galván, DJ Ruby, Martín García, Baunder, Elio Riso and Javier Bussola.

Parlagreco has also been part of the Buenos Aires agency Groove Selections, where he developed a residency that helped strengthen his presence within the underground electronic circuit.

As a producer, his music is available on platforms such as Beatport, Spotify and SoundCloud, where he shares his sonic identity through original tracks and DJ sets.

With a solid background and a refined sound, Parlagreco continues expanding his international presence, bringing his groove-driven and hypnotic sets to dancefloors across Europe and South America.
