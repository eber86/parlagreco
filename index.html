Artist profile

Biography

Hailing from Morón (Buenos Aires), Parlagreco is an Argentine DJ and producer with over two decades in the electronic scene. Blending Techno and Progressive House through deep grooves and hypnotic atmospheres, he has performed across South America and Europe (Barcelona, Amsterdam, Milan, Copenhagen) alongside top underground artists. Stream his music on Beatport, Spotify, and SoundCloud.
